Map My Risk

For anyone holding cryptocurrency. Twenty quick questions about how visible you are, how quickly your crypto could be moved, and who around you could be used as leverage. At the end you get a risk band, a profile, real 2026 cases that may reflect your profile, and the changes that may help you.

Private by design. Nothing you choose is seen by me. For the most privacy, download this tool and use it offline.

Built on my coding of 2026 wrench attack cases from Jameson Lopp's database, cross-checked against published research (GART Research 2026; Ordekian et al. 2024). Where sources disagree, the tool uses the more cautious figure. This is an awareness tool, not a prediction.

Most reported cases come from Europe, North America and parts of Asia, so dollar amounts reflect those settings. Judge amounts against incomes where you live.

This tool should not be solely relied upon to build your security architecture. Please do your own research and consult professionals if desired.
